# The gap is problem-solving *process*, not knowledge

Andrei is comfortable with TypeScript/JS syntax and can stitch snippets into working
systems, but cannot reliably derive a solution end-to-end without hints or copy-paste.
The mission is therefore *process*, not language or algorithm knowledge.

Implication: teach a repeatable derivation method (UDPCT — Understand, Do-by-hand,
Pattern, Code, Test) and drill it on *simple, already-understood* problems first, so
working memory goes to the method rather than to the problem. Escalate to unfamiliar
problems only once the method is fluent. Do NOT front-load new algorithms — that would
reintroduce the very knowledge-load we're trying to keep clear. See [[MISSION.md]].
